Delete your FishIn account

In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. Deletion is immediate.

No longer have the app? Email the address shown on FishIn's Google Play page, from the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your FishIn account. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username and photo
  • Spots you shared, catches you posted, your likes and comments
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Your FishIn Pro record on our server and your AI usage counts

What is not affected

  • Spots, catches and tackle saved only on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app.
  • A FishIn Pro subscription is billed by Google Play. Cancel it in the Play Store under Subscriptions, or it keeps renewing.
